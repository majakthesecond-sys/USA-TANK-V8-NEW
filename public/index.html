Tip

Armor & shells are simplified but fun:
  • AP = steady damage
  • HE = splash damage (good vs light tanks)
  • HEAT = strong vs armor, slower
  • APFSDS = fastest + best penetration (modern)
  • Switch ammo: press 1–4 for a type, or Q to cycle
  • Armor stress = repeated hits on one zone raise penetration and critical odds
  • The amber strip under an enemy HP bar shows its current armor stress
